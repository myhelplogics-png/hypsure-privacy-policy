Privacy Policy

com.aistudio.bropatch.hserv
Last Updated: Oct 6, 2026

Welcome to Bropatch ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when using our mobile application (the "Application").

1. Information We Collect

To provide core application functionality, Bropatch collects the following information:

  • Precise & Approximate Location: We request location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to render interactive map overlays via OpenStreetMap / osmdroid and enable location-based features.
  • Personal Account Details: When you sign in via Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, or Supabase, we process your email address, display name, user ID, and profile details.
  • Device & Push Notification Identifiers: To send real-time alerts via OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), we collect unique device tokens and network state info (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • Displaying your current position on the map interface.
  • Authenticating your user account securely.
  • Sending push notifications, updates, and service announcements.
  • Preventing security threats and fraud using Firebase App Check / ReCAPTCHA.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Application integrates trusted third-party service providers. Please review their individual privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Auth
  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • OneSignal Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time. If you wish to delete your account and associated records:

  • You can initiate account deletion within the app settings menu.
  • Or contact us directly at support@bropatch.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". All associated data will be removed within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Bropatch does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.
